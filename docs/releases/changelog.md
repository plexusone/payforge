## [v0.1.0] - 2026-10-28

### Highlights

- A billing runtime that reads a product's pricing and packaging from a pricing spec: it computes the products and prices a provider should hold, starts checkout and the customer portal, and turns a paid subscription into an entitlement through ports the host supplies
- Fails at startup when billing is half configured, and runs every account on the spec's default plan when no provider is configured
- A Stripe adapter for customers, plan and one-time checkout, the customer portal, Connect Express sellers with destination-charge sales, catalog sync with a dry run, and webhook verification
- A lifecycle that decides entitlement from the provider's current state, so replays and the order webhooks arrive in do not matter, and applies refunds, seller account changes and disputes through sinks

### Added

- `Billing` port and `Disabled` free mode: with no provider every account is on the spec's default plan and anything that needs a provider reports it is not configured ([`260f1f3`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/260f1f3))
- `CheckStartup` requires a valid spec, a webhook secret (two with a marketplace) and something to sell when a provider key is set, and names what is missing without ever printing a secret ([`4746a52`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/4746a52))
- `ProviderPlanFor` computes the products and prices a provider should hold, with stable lookup keys and a `payforge_app` tag so several products can share one account ([`639c2ff`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/639c2ff))
- Product checkout, customers, errors and the subscription grant rule: `active`, `trialing` and `past_due` grant, and a price the spec does not have never grants ([`f7e8b64`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/f7e8b64))
- `stripe` adapter with customers keyed to the host's subject, plan and one-time checkout, and customer portal sessions; idempotency keys cover every parameter Stripe compares ([`bfeb668`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/bfeb668))
- Catalog sync to a Stripe account by lookup key, with a dry run; it never deletes and a changed amount creates a new price and moves the key to it ([`e0e7fd7`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/e0e7fd7))
- Webhook verification against several signing secrets, reduced to a normalized event; `Webhook.Mode` is required and events of the other mode are ignored ([`fa5e779`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/fa5e779))
- Store and entitlement-sink ports with in-memory implementations ([`a80eb82`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/a80eb82))
- `Lifecycle.Handle` turns subscription events into entitlements from the provider's current state, idempotent by event, skipping connected-account events and keeping events for unknown customers retryable ([`a1bebb2`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/a1bebb2))
- Connect Express accounts, onboarding links, account state and destination-charge sales with a platform fee; a sale refuses a seller that cannot take charges or that this app did not create ([`3bed862`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/3bed862))
- Refunds of payforge sales and seller account changes applied through optional sale and seller sinks ([`21660f3`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/21660f3))
- Checkout, portal and onboarding URLs must be https (or http for localhost) and are limited to `AllowedURLHosts` ([`acbf1fd`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/acbf1fd))
- Prices without a lookup key grant a plan only when listed in `Lifecycle.LegacyPrices`; metadata never names a plan ([`d83ca60`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/d83ca60))
- Disputes and failed delayed payments become events, and disputes reach an optional `Lifecycle.Disputes` sink ([`c864306`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/c864306))
- Optional `Locker` port serializes the lifecycle and plan checkout per account, with `MemoryLocker` for a single process; a new plan checkout expires the subject's other open ones ([`4aa3c1f`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/4aa3c1f))
- Examples of a catalog sync, a pricing page, a webhook endpoint and limit checks, each run by a test without a Stripe account ([`d23e5a1`](https://github.com/plexusone/payforge/commit/d23e5a1))
